The Treaty at a Glance

Thirty articles, three concerns: who is taxed (Articles 1–5 define the scope and threshold), what is taxed and how (Articles 6–23 distribute taxing rights for each income type), and how relief works (Articles 24–30 prevent abuse and provide remedies). Click any article to study it in depth. Articles tagged CALC have a working calculator. Articles tagged CORE are the heaviest-tested in CA Final and most relevant in practice.

Pillar I · Source Rules

Allocate Taxing Rights

Where does this income arise? Each article tells one country it must yield some (or all) of its taxing right. Withholding caps in Articles 10–12 are the most cited example.

Pillar II · Residence Rules

Resolve Dual Status

If the same person is "resident" in both countries, Article 4's tiebreaker cascade decides — permanent home, then centre of vital interests, then habitual abode, then nationality, then MAP.

Pillar III · Relief Mechanism

Eliminate Double Tax

Even when both countries can tax, Article 25 commits the residence country to give credit for the source country's tax — capped at the residence country's tax on that income (Section 90 / IRC §901).
